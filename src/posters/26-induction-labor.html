Amniotomy + oxytocin

ACOG CPG 8 (2024) pp.152–154, Table 3 · AHRQ Labor Dystocia (2020) Table 67 · NICE NG207 §1.7.5 · CRICO OB Guideline 15 (2026) · AHRQ Safe Oxytocin tool (2023)
Amniotomy shortens induced / augmented labor. Confirm presentation, a stable, well-applied presenting part and placental site. High / mobile head → cord prolapse risk: FM attending assesses first. FHR before and immediately after; sudden fall → examine for cord 29 · A Document ROM time + fluid.

E

Too many contractions?

ACOG CPG 10 (2025) Tables 1–2, pp.589–590, 594–595
> 5 / 10 minTachysystole, averaged over 30 min. Assess FHR, resting time, context 28 · E Also assess prolonged contractions and incomplete relaxation. Concerning FHR → respond now; do not wait to complete the 30‑min average.
  1. Reduce / stop induction agents; withhold further miSOPROStol; remove dinoprostone insert.
  2. Call FM attending; Category III → + OB/GYN to the bedside now, alert anesthesia + neonatal team 3 · A Hypertonus after neuraxial 29 · B
  3. Persistent Category III or high-risk II after oxytocin off → uterine relaxant: terbutaline 0.25 mg SUBQ Local per approved order. Category III not resolving → expedite delivery; relaxant never delays it.
  4. Resume only when resolved + FHR reassuring, per restart orders.
