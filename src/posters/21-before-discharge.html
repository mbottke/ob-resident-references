Home only when all are met

  • Maternal reassessment: repeat vital signs + symptoms reviewed after observation / treatment; abnormalities addressed; symptoms manageable with home plan. Oral fluids / medicines tolerated when relevant.

D

Get help now — do not wait for the next visit

CDC HEAR HER (2024) · show and read with the patient · postpartum bleeding thresholds 6
Any of these, now or after you go home → get care right away Now
  • Headache: severe, won’t go away or getting worse
  • Fainting or dizziness that won’t go away
  • Changes in vision
  • Fever: 100.4 °F / 38 °C or higher
  • Marked swelling of face or hands
  • Thoughts of harming yourself or the baby
  • Trouble breathing
  • Chest discomfort or racing / irregular heartbeat
  • Severe vomiting or can’t keep fluids down
  • Severe belly pain that won’t go away
  • Baby moving less or stopped moving
  • Bleeding more than spotting, leaking fluid or bad-smelling discharge
  • Painful, red or swollen arm or leg, especially one side
  • Sudden overwhelming weakness or tiredness

F

Make the home plan workable substance use 24 · B–D

CDC (2024) · ACOG CS 23 (2025) · CO 518 (2012) · CS 11 (2024) · ACOG/ASAM CO 711 (2017)
No prenatal care
Confirm best dating / available records; review missing prenatal evaluation; obtain indicated HIV, HBV, HCV and syphilis testing. Identify receiving prenatal clinician; assign pending results.
Language · access
Ask preferred spoken / written language and communication needs. Qualified medical interpreter; understandable translated instructions / accommodations.
Safety · IPV
Intimate partner violence (IPV): speak with patient alone; professional interpreter. Explain confidentiality limits before screening. Assess safe destination / contact; offer advocacy + patient-directed safety plan.
Transport · resources
Confirm ride home + return route, pharmacy / medicine access, housing, food, telephone. Barriers that defeat necessary outpatient monitoring → escalate for an achievable disposition.
Substance use
Nonjudgmental verbal screen; assess intoxication / withdrawal + immediate safety. Continue prescribed treatment; offer linkage. Toxicology testing requires informed consent + explanation of implications.

G

Teach-back before departure

ACOG CO 676 (2016) · prompts are proposed implementation
Ask the patient to explain in their own words:
  • “What did today’s evaluation show — and what remains uncertain?”
  • “Which symptoms mean return now? Where will you go?”
  • “How will you take medicines / check BP or movement if prescribed?”
  • “When is follow-up? Who will contact you about pending results?”
Plain words + interpreter as needed. Gaps → re-explain → repeat teach-back. Give matching written instructions and a working contact number.
