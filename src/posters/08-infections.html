Recurrent UTI: before prophylaxis

Fully treat the current episode. Correct reversible causes: resistance, adherence, obstruction, stones, retention; renal US or urology as indicated.

Suppression / prophylaxis

Nitrofurantoin 100 mg or cephalexin 250–500 mg PO nightly (or 1 post-coital dose), by susceptibility. Nitrofurantoin: T1 only if no alternative; switch to cephalexin by 38w0d (earlier if labor imminent). Stop at delivery.

Avoid Stop

In pregnancy: fluoroquinolones and tetracyclines.
For pyelo / possible upper tract: nitrofurantoin or fosfomycin.
Empiric treatment: amoxicillin or ampicillin.

Screen by culture

Dipstick is not an ASB screen. Symptomatic: pyuria (> 5–10 WBC/hpf or LE+) sensitive, nonspecific; nitrite specific (~94–98%), insensitive; both negative (NPV 78–98%) doesn’t replace culture.
Midstream clean catch: ~⅓ grow skin flora up to 10⁴ — mixed or low-count skin flora usually = contamination. 10⁵ CFU/mL = 10⁸ CFU/L: confirm lab units.
B

Sexually transmitted infectionsNo doxycycline in pregnancy. Test for HIV, syphilis, GC/CT with any STI. Abstain 7 d after treatment and until partners treated.

CDC STI Treatment Guidelines 2021 (current Nov 2026)
ACOG PA Syphilis Screening 2024 (reaff. 2025)
Chlamydia
Azithromycin 1 g PO once
Alternative: amoxicillin 500 mg PO TID × 7 d

Test of cure (NAAT) ~4 wk after treatment, then retest at 3 mo.

Partners: last 60 d.

Gonorrhea
Ceftriaxone 500 mg IM once 1 g if ≥ 150 kg
+ azithromycin 1 g PO once unless chlamydia excluded

Cephalosporin allergy → ID / STI consult (gentamicin: caution in pregnancy).

Pharyngeal: test of cure 7–14 d. All: retest 3 mo.

Trichomonas
Metronidazole 500 mg PO BID × 7 d
Male partner: metronidazole 2 g PO once

Dx: NAAT (wet mount sensitivity 44–68%). No tinidazole in pregnancy.

Retest 3 mo; recurrence NAAT ≥ 3 wk after treatment.

Expedited partner therapy

CDC: use where legal when partners can’t be seen. GC partner: cefixime 800 mg PO once. If CT not excluded — doxycycline add-on for a nonpregnant partner: 100 mg BID × 7 d. Not for syphilis.

Screen & follow

Screen
All: 1st visit · 3rd trimester (28 wk) · delivery 7 · 01; any fetal death > 20 wk
Repeat titers
Treatment at ≤ 24 wk: repeat titers ≥ 8 wk after treatment (e.g., 32 wk) and at delivery. Treatment at > 24 wk: repeat titers at delivery.
Concerning
response
Fourfold rise sustained > 2 wk = failure or reinfection. Treatment is likely inadequate if delivery occurs < 30 d after therapy.

Second half of pregnancy

Fetal ultrasound for congenital syphilis (don’t delay treatment). Jarisch-Herxheimer can trigger preterm labor or fetal distress: report fever, contractions, ↓ fetal movement.

Cochrane (Mackeen) 2015

Postpartum endometritis fever + uterine tenderness ± foul lochia
Clindamycin 900 mg IV q8h+ gentamicin 5 mg/kg IV q24h (or 1.5 mg/kg q8h)

Add ampicillin if no response by 48–72 h or enterococcus suspected. Adjust gentamicin for renal function.

Stop
Afebrile 24–48 h and clinically improved → stop IV. No oral course after uncomplicated endometritis.
No response
No improvement after 48–72 h → exam, imaging, cultures. Assess for wound infection, abscess or hematoma, retained products, septic pelvic thrombophlebitis, or resistant organism.

Influenza — pregnancy or ≤ 2 wk postpartum = high risk

Suspected illnessTreat now, any trimester; don’t wait for the test (best ≤ 48 h of onset): oseltamivir 75 mg PO BID × 5 d
Post-exposureAsymptomatic, ≤ 48 h after exposure: oseltamivir 75 mg PO daily × 7 d after last exposure
No baloxavir in pregnancy or lactation. Renal-adjust.
