Bilirubin≥ 35 wk · use BiliTool

AAP 2022 CPG
Pediatrics 2022;150:e2022058859
  • Jaundice < 24 h → transcutaneous (TcB) or total serum bilirubin (TSB) now. Everyone: TcB or TSB at 24–48 h or before discharge. Discharge bilirubin timing 25 · F
  • TSB if TcB ≥ threshold − 3 mg/dL or ≥ 15. Use total bilirubin; don’t subtract direct.
  • Neurotoxicity risk factors (lower thresholds): GA < 38 wk · albumin < 3.0 · DAT+ / G6PD / hemolysis · sepsis · instability in past 24 h.
  • TSB ≥ phototherapy threshold → start phototherapy. Record start time and next TSB. Setup and irradiance check: ____

Escalation of care

TSB ≥ exchange threshold − 2 mg/dL → STAT labs, IV fluids, intensive phototherapy, TSB q2h, call NICU. Transport activation 23 · B
Consider IVIG 0.5–1 g/kg for DAT+ isoimmune hemolysis only when TSB ≥ escalation-of-care threshold and rising despite intensive phototherapy (benefit uncertain; possible NEC risk).
  • Stop phototherapy once ≥ 2 mg/dL below starting threshold; rebound check per BiliTool. No water or dextrose water.
