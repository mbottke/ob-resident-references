Unstable? No D-dimer/scores

2026 AHA/ACC multisociety PE guideline · ASH 2018 recs 7–8 · Activase label

Arrest → CPR, left uterine displacement. Fundus ≥ umbilicus: prepare resuscitative delivery now; no ROSC → aim birth by 5 min 2 · D. Suspected PE: lysis may be considered during CPR.

03

Leg: compression ultrasound

ASH 2018 rec 31 · ACOG PB 196 · Chan, CMAJ 2013 · Le Gal (EDVIGE), BMJ 2012
First test
Proximal or whole-leg CUS, both with iliac imaging. Pregnancy DVT is mostly left-sided, proximal.
Iliac clue
Whole-leg swelling, buttock, flank or back pain → iliac Doppler or MRV even with clear femoral veins.
Positive
Treat → panel 05.
Negative, still suspected
Serial CUS (days 3 and 7) or MRV, no gadolinium.
Negative, low suspicion
Stop DVT testing only. If evaluating PE, continue its workup.
Companion note · evidence and alternative (not a step in YEARS)

Artemis: 3-mo VTE after rule-out 0.21%; CTPA avoided in 39% (65% 1st trimester, 32% 3rd).

Alternative pathway — use instead of YEARS (CT-PE-Pregnancy): revised Geneva low (0–3) or intermediate (4–10) + D-dimer < 500 → excluded; otherwise bilateral CUS: proximal DVT → treat; negative → CTPA. 0% 3-mo VTE (95% CI 0–1.0%).

04

Chest imaging

ASH 2018 rec 30 · 2026 AHA/ACC (2a) · ACOG PB 196 · CO 723
Pregnant: leg symptoms → CUS first → panel 03; otherwise chest x-ray first. Postpartum: CTPA.
CXR normal

CXR abnormal
CTPA
CXR normal: V/Q, perfusion-only first (ASH prefers) or low-dose CTPA (AHA/ACC 2026, 2a) — use the faster test.
Nondiagnostic V/Q or CTPA → the other test, or CUS if not done. Negative CUS does not exclude PE.
Waiting for imaging? Start therapeutic LMWH now and continue until PE is excluded, unless bleeding risk forbids (RCOG 37b); birth, neuraxial or lysis near → IV UFH → panel 05.
  • Fetal dose from either test ≪ 50 mGy; no anomalies, FGR or loss reported below it (CO 723). Don’t withhold imaging.
  • Iodinated contrast is acceptable in pregnancy; breastfeeding continues (CO 723).

07

Postpartum & breastfeeding

ASH 2018 recs 12–13 · LactMed (NLM, 2026) · ACCP 2012
  • Compatible: LMWH, UFH, fondaparinux, warfarin (INR 2–3; overlap parenteral ≥ 5 d + INR ≥ 2 for 24 h).
  • DOACs: avoid while breastfeeding (ASH strong rec). LactMed: apixaban milk levels high; rivaroxaban, dabigatran low, data limited. Not breastfeeding → DOAC OK.
  • Contraception: progestin-only or nonhormonal; CHC is MEC 3 on therapeutic anticoagulation for acute VTE (MEC 4 early postpartum) 6 · C
08

Not PE? Think of

SMFM AFE checklist 2026 · ESC HFA PPCM 2010 · AHA SCAD statement 2018
PPCM
Late pregnancy to months postpartum; orthopnea, edema; BNP, echo: LVEF < 45%.
ACS / SCAD
Chest pain: ECG, troponin; SCAD is the leading cause of pregnancy-associated MI, mostly postpartum.
AFE
Sudden hypoxia + hypotension or arrest, ± DIC, in labor or ≤ 30 min after placenta → code 2 · D
Workup
ECG, troponin, BNP or NT-proBNP, CXR, SpO₂ / ABG, echo if hypoxic, hypotensive or RV strain suspected.
Others
Pneumonia or aspiration, pulmonary edema of preeclampsia 2 · B, sepsis 13 · 01, asthma, anemia.
