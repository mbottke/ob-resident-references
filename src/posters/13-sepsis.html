01

Recognize

CMQCC Obstetric Sepsis Toolkit v2.0 (2025) · SMFM Consult #67 (2023) · SSC 2026
Pregnancy-adjusted screen applies ≥ 20 wk to ≤ 72 h postpartum. Outside that window use the standard adult screen; unmodified SIRS fires in most intraamniotic infections.
Fever is neither necessary nor sufficient. Unexplained organ dysfunction + possible infection = sepsis until proved otherwise (SMFM). 13 · 02
Step 1 · serious-infection screen
  • Oral temp < 36 °C or ≥ 38 °C
  • HR > 110 on recheck ≤ 20 min
  • RR > 24
  • WBC > 15,000 or < 4,000/µL, or > 10% bands
  • Vitals ≤ 2 h apart; WBC ≤ 24 h
Possible infection + ≥ 2 criteria above → bedside evaluation ≤ 30 minSerious infection → antibiotics + modest fluid challenge now; Step 2 labs in parallel
Step 2 · possible infection + any 1 → sepsis
  • SBP < 85, MAP < 65, or SBP fall > 40
  • New CPAP / BiPAP or ventilation; SpO₂ < 92% or new O₂ need
  • Urine output < 60 mL over 2 h
  • Appears toxic, confused or agitated
  • Cr ≥ 1.2 mg/dL or doubled · bilirubin > 2 mg/dL
  • Platelets < 100,000 or INR > 1.5 or aPTT > 60 s
Sepsis → first-hour bundle now 13 · 02
Septic shockafter fluids, vasopressors needed to keep MAP ≥ 65 and lactate > 2 mmol/L.
  • Lactate: > 2 mmol/L abnormal outside labor; in labor > 4 is concerning. Don’t draw in 2nd stage or ≤ 1 h after birth unless very ill. Marks severity, not organ injury.
  • qSOFA (SBP ≤ 100, RR ≥ 22, altered mentation): ≈ 50% sensitive in maternal sepsis, not validated in pregnancy — never the only screen.
  • Mimics: hemorrhage, preeclampsia, PE, amniotic fluid embolism, cardiomyopathy, DKA, adrenal crisis, pancreatitis, anaphylaxis. Use 6 · A early-warning triggers postpartum.

04

Source control

In parallel from time zeroSMFM #67 recs 9, 18
  • Uterus is the source → deliver or evacuate promptly, at any gestational age (intraamniotic infection, septic abortion, retained tissue, previable PPROM with infection).
  • Otherwise sepsis alone is not an indication for delivery — deliver for obstetric indications once the mother is stabilizing.
  • Image early: abscess → drain (percutaneous first); necrotizing infection → debridement, hysterectomy if myometrial necrosis; remove infected lines. Higher-level care 27 · E / F
05

The fetus

SMFM #67 · ACOG CPG 10 (2025)
  • Resuscitate the mother first — restoring maternal perfusion is intrauterine resuscitation. Monitor once she is stabilizing, if viable (at the limit of viability, per family wishes).
  • Fetal tachycardia, minimal variability, late decelerations usually reflect maternal fever, hypoxemia, hypotension or acidosis: correct those, then re-read the strip 3 · A 29 · D before deciding on delivery.
  • Antenatal steroids are not contraindicated by sepsis or hydrocortisone. Call neonatology early.
06

Reassess & follow up

SMFM #67 recs 13, 16, 17 · CMQCC v2.0 · SSC 2021
Each bolus
Dynamic measures (passive leg raise, pulse-pressure variation, POCUS), lungs, SpO₂.
Daily
Cultures, source, antibiotic de-escalation · VTE pharmacologic prophylaxis in shock · insulin if glucose > 180 mg/dL.
Discharge
Survivor follow-up: physical, cognitive, mood; postpartum review 6 · A
