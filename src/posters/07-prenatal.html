Genetic screening

cfDNA our test: Panorama — offered to all; no routine microdeletion screen. Positive or no-call → genetic counseling, detailed US, offer CVS / amniocentesis (a screen is not a diagnosis). Diagnostic testing is an option for everyone. Carrier screening our test: Horizon — cystic fibrosis + SMA to all; fragile X by history.

Rh D-negative, no immune anti-D 1 · 14

At 28 wk: antibody screen and RhIG 300 mcg. Newborn Rh+: RhIG again ≤ 72 h after birth. < 12w0d abortion or loss: ACOG suggests forgoing routine RhIG (SMFM disagrees).

Diabetes screening 12

ACOG PB 190 + CPU 2024 · USPSTF 2021

First visit: screen for overt diabetes, not GDM

BMI ≥ 25 (≥ 23 Asian American) plus ≥ 1: first-degree relative with DM, prior GDM, prediabetes, high-risk ethnicity, HTN, CVD, HDL < 35 or TG > 250, PCOS, inactivity, acanthosis, age ≥ 35, HIV. Overt DM: A1c ≥ 6.5%, FPG ≥ 126 or 75 g 2-h ≥ 200; confirm with a 2nd abnormal result unless hyperglycemia is unequivocal. Prediabetes still rescreens at 24–28 wk.
Universal at 24w0d–28w6d. Two-step preferred by ACOG; one-step acceptable.

04

Aspirin 162 mg

Hospital protocol · ACOG/SMFM PA 2021 · USPSTF 2021
Give for any 1 high-risk factor: prior preeclampsia · multifetal · chronic HTN · type 1/2 DM · kidney disease · SLE / APS
Or for > 1 moderate-risk factor: nulliparity · BMI > 30 · family hx preeclampsia · Black race · lower income · age ≥ 35 · prior LBW/SGA or other adverse outcome · > 10-yr interval · IVF. Consider either alone: Black race (proxy for racism), lower income.
Local 162 mg PO daily (hospital protocol; ACOG/USPSTF cite 81 mg). Start 12–28 wk (ideally < 16), until delivery.
05

Preterm birth prevention

ACOG PB 234 · PA 2023 · SMFM CS #70 (2024)
  • Singleton, prior spontaneous PTB < 37 wk: serial transvaginal cervical length (TVCL) 16w0d–24w0d; vaginal progesterone if cervix short. Prior sPTB < 34 wk + CL < 25 mm before 24 wk → consider cerclage.
  • Asymptomatic singleton, no prior PTB, TVCL ≤ 20 mm before 24w0d: vaginal progesterone. Same population, TVCL 21–25 mm before 24w0d → consider vaginal progesterone (shared decision).
  • 17-OHPC no longer recommended (Makena withdrawn 2023).
