Pain — multimodal

Scheduled NSAID + acetaminophen (separate products, not combinations). Add low-dose short-acting oral opioid only if needed; discharge quantity by shared decision (median after cesarean ≈ 20 tablets). No codeine or tramadol if breastfeeding. OUD / MOUD: analgesia plan 24 · D

F

Lactation, vaccines & labs

ABM Protocol #36 (2022) · ACIP · ADA 2026
ACOG PB 181 · LactMed (NLM)

Mastitis spectrum (ABM 2022)

Feed on demand; avoid deep breast massage and overpumping. Ice, ibuprofen 800 mg q8h, acetaminophen. Antibiotics only if bacterial: dicloxacillin or cephalexin 500 mg QID × 10–14 d (clindamycin if allergic). Milk culture if severe, recurrent or no better at 48 h. Abscess → drain. Keep breastfeeding.

Medications in milk

Most are compatible (relative infant dose < 10% generally acceptable). Check LactMed (NLM). MOUD / THC and milk 24 · E
Adequate intake: 8–12 feeds/day; by day 5 ≥ 6 wet diapers 10 · 03.

Vaccines & labs

RhIG
Unsensitized Rh−, infant Rh+ or unknown: 300 mcg ≤ 72 h; missed → give now (some benefit to 28 d). Kleihauer–Betke / flow → extra doses.
Vaccines
MMR, varicella if nonimmune — don’t delay for RhIG. Tdap if not given in pregnancy and never received before; otherwise check catch-up indications.
GDM
75 g OGTT 4–12 wk or before discharge.
