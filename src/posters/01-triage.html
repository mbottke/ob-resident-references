Vaginal bleeding if < 20 wk, use 9

Stop No digital exam until placenta location known
Stabilize2 large-bore IVs if heavy · CBC, fibrinogen, PT/PTT, type & screen (crossmatch if heavy) · Kleihauer–Betke (KB) / flow if Rh− · continuous EFM
UltrasoundPlacenta, presentation, FHR, fluid · limited OB US 76815 · TV 76817
Previa
Painless, bright red. Dx by US (TVUS safe). Deliver at 36w0d–37w6d if uncomplicated.
Abruption
Painful, tender/hypertonic uterus, frequent ctx, Cat II/III, DIC. Clinical dx — normal US doesn’t exclude. Serial fibrinogen. < 34 wk: steroids if time, magnesium if < 32 wk. Choose the delivery route by clinical status, not GA. No tocolytics.
Vasa previa
ROM → painless bleeding → fetal brady / sinusoidal → emergent CS. 29 · EKnown: planned CS 34w0d–37w0d; steroids 28–32 wk. ROM at viable GA → cesarean.
Also: labor / bloody show, uterine rupture, cervicitis, ectropion, polyp, cancer, postcoital; urinary or rectal source.

Trauma

EAST 2025 · SOGC 2015 · AHA 2025
  • Mother first — primary survey; manual left uterine displacement if fundus ≥ umbilicus.
  • Arrest → resuscitative hysterotomy by 5 min after arrest 2 · D
  • Imaging / O₂: don’t withhold indicated imaging (< 50 mGy not linked to anomaly/loss). O₂ for maternal hypoxemia (not as fetal resuscitation).
  • Labs: CBC, type & antibody screen, PT/PTT, fibrinogen, KB if Rh− (some centers test all). RhIG if Rh− 1 · 14
  • EFM ≥ 4–6 h if viable — abruption can declare hours later.

Extend to 24 h if any

Contractions > 1 / 10 min · uterine tenderness or pain · vaginal bleeding · ROM · abnormal FHR · high-risk mechanism · fibrinogen < 200 mg/dL · serious maternal injury
Risks: abruption, uterine rupture, fetomaternal hemorrhage, preterm labor, direct fetal injury, pelvic fracture.
!
Suspected uterine rupture with bradycardia → emergent laparotomy. No tocolysis.

03

High blood pressure

ACOG PB 222 (reaff. 2026) · PB 203 · CHAP PA · CO 831
Severe rangeSBP ≥ 160 or DBP ≥ 110 2 · B persistent ≥ 15 min → treat within 30–60 min
Ask: headache, visual change, RUQ/epigastric pain, dyspnea. Edema is not a criterion.
Workup: CBC, Cr, AST/ALT, urine protein:creatinine (P:C), LDH (if HELLP suspected) · NST ± BPP · US growth/fluid.

Classify

Chronic HTNBefore pregnancy or < 20 wk. Treat at ≥ 140/90 (CHAP).
Gestational HTNSBP ≥ 140 or DBP ≥ 90 on 2 readings ≥ 4 h apart, after 20 wk; no proteinuria, no severe features.
PreeclampsiaBP criteria + either proteinuria (24 h ≥ 300 mg, P:C ≥ 0.3, or dipstick 2+ only if neither available) or any new end-organ feature below, even without proteinuria.
With severe features (SF) — any one:
  • SBP ≥ 160 or DBP ≥ 110
  • plt < 100,000/µL
  • Cr > 1.1 mg/dL or doubled
  • pulmonary edema
  • visual disturbance
  • new headache unresponsive to meds
  • AST/ALT > 2× ULN or severe RUQ/epigastric pain
Proteinuria amount is not one.
HELLPLDH ≥ 600 IU/L · AST and ALT > 2× ULN · plt < 100,000/µL. Up to 15% lack HTN or proteinuria; 30% postpartum.
EclampsiaNew seizure with no other cause — can be the first sign.

Deliver at

Deliver at any GA: uncontrolled BP, eclampsia, HELLP, pulmonary edema, abruption, rising Cr, neuro signs, abnormal fetal testing.

Rupture of membranes

ACOG PB 217 (reaff. 2026) · SMFM #71 · PA 2025
History: time, color (clear / bloody / meconium), intercourse, contractions, fever.
Speculum (SSE)Sterile; look for pooling, no lubricant. No digital exam unless active labor or imminent birth.
NitrazineFluid pH 7.1–7.3 vs vaginal 3.8–4.5. False-positive: blood, semen, BV, trichomonas.
FernExamine dried fluid under a microscope.
PAMG-1 / IGFBP-1Use as an adjunct; not alone (FDA).
US / dyeAFI adjunct · dye amnioinfusion: definitive.
Fetal fibronectin (fFN) is not a ROM test: swab first, run only after ROM excluded.

Watch for intraamniotic infection

Either T ≥ 39.0 °C; or T 38.0–38.9 °C + any 1 of: fetal HR > 160 for 10 min · WBC > 15,000 (no steroids) · purulent fluid. Signs without fever also count. → broad-spectrum antibiotics and delivery. Regimens 3 · B · IAI

Term PROM ≥ 37w0d

Recommend induction (oxytocin) 26 · D; expectant 12–24 h reasonable if reassuring. GBS+ → intrapartum GBS prophylaxis (IAP) + induce now. GBS unknown 1 · 08 Local buccal misoprostol option.

PPROM < 37w0d — ≥ half deliver within 1 wk

20w0d–23w6dMFM + neonatology counseling 27 · E; abortion care or expectant. Expectant maternal morbidity 57–60%. Abx may be considered. Steroids (ACS) from 22w0d, magnesium from 23w0d, only if active neonatal care planned + desired. GBS IAP from 23w0d.
24w0d–33w6dAdmit, expectant · latency abx · ACS (diabetes no barrier) · magnesium neuroprotection < 32w0d 1 · 06 · GBS IAP if indicated 1 · 08 · deliver for infection, abruption, abnormal testing.
34w0d–36w6dDeliver or expectant, not past 37w0d. No latency abx, no tocolysis. ACS only if no prior course, delivery planned ≥ 24 h and ≤ 7 d away, no chorio, no pregestational DM.

Latency antibiotics — 7 days

First 48 h IV
Ampicillin 2 g IV q6h
+ erythromycin 250 mg IV q6h
Then 5 days PO
Amoxicillin 250 mg PO q8h
+ erythromycin base 333 mg PO q8h
Azithromycin 1 g PO may replace erythromycin. No amox-clav (NEC).
PPROM risk factors: prior PPROM, short cervix, 2nd/3rd-trimester bleeding, low BMI, low SES, smoking, illicit drugs.

Labor?

ACOG CPG 8 (2024) · CO 766
  • Fluid? → speculum (SSE) first 1 · 04 Bleeding? → placenta first.
  • Regular contractions + cervical change (preterm: or ≥ 2 cm at presentation). Bedside US for presentation.
  • Active phase starts at 6 cm. 26 · F Latent > 16 h (nullip) may be called prolonged — not arrest. Slow progress 26 · G
Recheck in ≈ 2 h for cervical change. Admit to our service only ≥ 35w0d; earlier → call OB 27 · B
Before home 21: shared home-coping plan + return precautions. Admission may still be right for pain or fatigue.

Preterm labor

ACOG PB 171 · CO 713 · CO 455 · SMFM #58 · PA 2021 · fFN insert
Evaluate: confirm GA · EFM · contractions · SSE before digital exam (ROM, bleeding, fFN swab) · TVUS cervical length · UA/culture, GC/CT, wet prep, GBS swab.

Cervical length → fFN (24w0d–34w6d · intact membranes · dilation < 3 cm)

≥ 30 mmLow risk
20–29 mmSend fFN
< 20 mmHigh risk
Protocol cutoffs, not ACOG. fFN negative: NPV 99.5% at 7 d, 99.2% at 14 d. Positive: PPV only 13–17%.
Invalid: ROM, cerclage, moderate/gross bleeding, previa/abruption, lubricant or vaginal progesterone, digital exam / TVUS before swab.
Recent intercourse: < 24 h per label (many use 48 h); a negative result still counts.

Antenatal corticosteroids

Betamethasone 12 mg IM q24h × 2
or dexamethasone 6 mg IM q12h × 4
  • 24w0d–33w6d at risk of birth ≤ 7 d: recommended. 23 wk: may consider. 22 wk: only if resuscitation planned.
  • 34w0d–36w6d: offer if birth likely ≤ 7 d and before 37w0d, singleton, no prior course, no pregestational DM, no chorio. Don’t tocolyze for it. Warn NICU: hypoglycemia.
  • Diabetes is not a contraindication < 34 wk — give it, watch glucose.
  • Rescue (once): < 34w0d, birth ≤ 7 d, prior course ≥ 14 d ago (may consider from 7 d).

Magnesium neuroprotection — < 32w0d

Use one trial regimen unit-wide, e.g., BEAM: 6 g IV over 20–30 min → 2 g/h until birth or 12 h, whichever first; stop if birth no longer imminent; if imminent again, resume 2 g/h — re-load 6 g only if ≥ 6 h since stopping. Renal rules 2 · B. CP in survivors RR ≈ 0.55–0.71.

Tocolysis — ≤ 48 h, to give steroids / magnesium / transport 27 · E

Before tocolysis — contraindicated: intrauterine fetal death, lethal anomaly, abnormal fetal status, preeclampsia with severe features, unstable bleeding, chorioamnionitis.
NIFEdipine30 mg PO → 10–20 mg q4–6h (max 180 mg/day). Not: hypotension, preload-dependent cardiac lesion.
Indomethacin50–100 mg → 25–50 mg q4–6h; < 32 wk only. Not: bleeding/platelet disorder, renal or hepatic dz, ulcer, aspirin-sensitive asthma.
Terbutaline0.25 mg SUBQ q20–30 min, up to 4 doses. FDA boxed: ≤ 48–72 h, never oral. Not: cardiac dz, poorly controlled DM.
No maintenance tocolysis. Regimens vary — follow unit protocol.

Abdominal / flank pain

ACOG CO 723 · ACR 2022 · IDSA 2024 · ACG 2024
Exam abdomen + uterus; EFM if viable. Constant severe pain, peritoneal signs, shock or abnormal FHR → senior OB ± surgery.
RUQ / epigastricPreeclampsia / HELLP 1 · 03 · biliary → RUQ US · pancreatitis → lipase. CBC, Cr, AST/ALT, bilirubin ± P:C, LDH.
Right / focalAppendicitis → US, then MRI without contrast if nondiagnostic. Don’t delay indicated CT.
Flank ± feverUA + culture; pyelonephritis 8 · A
UterineTender, hypertonic ± bleeding → abruption 1 · 01. Sudden pain + fetal bradycardia, uterine scar → rupture: emergency OB.

Vomiting after 20 wk

RCOG GTG 69 (2024) · ADA/EASD 2024 · FIGO 2025
New → find another cause. BP, temp, RUQ pain, headache, jaundice, mentation · CBC, lytes, Cr, glucose, LFTs. Diabetes or ill → β-hydroxybutyrate + VBG: DKA can occur with glucose < 200 mg/dL 12 · 07
Hypoglycemia, jaundice, coagulopathy, AKI or confusion → AFLP: PT/INR, fibrinogen, MFM; stabilize, deliver. Poor intake → thiamine before dextrose (never delay glucose for hypoglycemia). Antiemetics 9 · F

GBS prophylaxis

ACOG CO 797 (reaff. 2025)
  • Give if: GBS+ (36w0d–37w6d culture) · GBS bacteriuria this pregnancy · prior infant with GBS disease.
  • Prelabor cesarean with intact membranes → no GBS-specific IAP; give routine cesarean surgical prophylaxis.
  • If GBS status is unknown, give IAP for any of: < 37w0d, ROM ≥ 18 h, T ≥ 38.0 °C, intrapartum NAAT+.
  • PPROM: give IAP despite latency abx.
  • Suspected IAI → broad-spectrum regimen instead 3 · B · IAI
