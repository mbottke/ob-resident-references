01

Assess a whole feed

ABM #2 (2022) · ABM #3 (2017) · AAP 2022
Lethargy, hard to wake or other illness red flags → escalate now 15 · A
  • Watch an entire feed before a non-urgent supplement: position, latch (mouth wide, chin to breast, nose clear), audible swallowing (= milk transfer), nipple pain.
  • 8–12 feeds / 24 h on cues. Sleepy after the first ≈ 2 h alert period and cluster feeding at night are normal — skin-to-skin, rouse for feeds, hand-express colostrum.
  • Every exam: % weight change + NEWT percentile, jaundice, voids and stools (count, color, urate crystals). Opioid exposure: ESC 24 · H
10% loss is not an automatic order for formula: evaluate the infant and the feed.

04

Supplement? Indications

ABM #3 Table 1 (2017 — still current Nov 2026)

Infant

  • Asymptomatic hypoglycemia confirmed by laboratory glucose, persisting despite frequent breastfeeding; treatment never waits for the lab 5 · B
  • Inadequate intake: dehydration · weight loss 02 · stools 03
  • Jaundice from suboptimal intake (day 2–5, ongoing loss, few stools) 5 · C
  • Inborn error of metabolism requiring specific macronutrients

Mother

  • Delayed secretory activation (72–120 h) with inadequate infant intake
  • Glandular insufficiency (< 5%) · breast surgery / pathology causing poor milk production
  • Maternal medication requiring interruption of breastfeeding
  • Mother–infant separation without available expressed milk
  • Intolerable pain despite help
Not indicated: feeding well with expected output and weight loss · sleepiness or cluster feeding alone · maternal fatigue alone (rooming out doesn’t add sleep).
Order: indication · content · volume · method; consent if not medically indicated.
05

How much to give

ABM #3 Table 2 (reference ranges) · ABM #10
Term values: average colostrum intake of healthy term breastfed infants. Feed to cues; limit to physiologic volume.
Method: cup, spoon, syringe, finger, at-breast supplementer or paced bottle — no method proven best; choose one that feeds the volume in 20–30 min.

07

Late preterm & early term

ABM #10 (2016) · AAP iron 2026 · 34w0d–36w6d and 37w0d–38w6d
Loss > 3% by 24 h or > 7% by day 3 (not the term 10%) → assess a feed ± test weights, low TcB threshold; supplement if needed, after breastfeeding: 5–10 mL/feed day 1 · 10–30 mL after, expressed milk first. Sleepy → expressed milk; breastfeed when alert.
  • Hand-express colostrum within 1 h of birth and ≈ q3h if small, sleepy or not latching; while supplementing, express after breastfeeds ≥ 6× / 24 h
  • 8–12 feeds / 24 h; wake if no cues by 4 h. Watch 12–24 h for instability; glucose 5 · B · nursery GA / weight limits 23 · B
  • Discharge: temp stable ≥ 24 h, normal RR, adequate intake documented (test weights or stable weight), preferably ≤ 7% loss, written feeding plan
  • After discharge: weigh weekly to 40 wk PMA; gain 20–30 g/day. < 20 g/day → change the plan, re-weigh daily or every 2–3 d
  • Iron, late preterm: total 2–3 mg/kg/day, starting by 2 wk of age
