06

Home BP monitoring

ACOG PB 203 · Severe HTN Bundle 2025 · ACC/AHA 2017 SMBP checklist
  • Validated upper-arm cuff (validatebp.org; if pregnant, one listed for pregnancy); bladder ≥ 80% of arm. Check once against office BP.
  • Seated, back supported, feet flat, arm at heart level; avoid caffeine and tobacco for ≥ 30 min before measuring; rest 5 min.
  • Morning before meds and evening: 2 readings, 1 min apart; record every reading.
  • Cuff + log: HDP discharged on meds; chronic HTN from the first prenatal visit.

07

After discharge: follow up, wean

ACOG CO 736 (reaff. 2025) · PB 203 · PB 222
  • BP check ≤ 72 h after discharge if severe HTN; by 7–10 d after birth for every HDP 6 · A.
  • BP dips after birth, then often peaks days 3–6 and may stay up through weeks 1–2; postpartum preeclampsia can present up to 6 wk. An early rapid fall suggests blood loss.
  • Wean one drug at a time; reduce labetalol gradually. Hypertension persisting > 12 wk postpartum → reclassify as chronic HTN.
  • Any HDP = lifelong cardiovascular risk: primary-care handoff, yearly BP. Discharge BP plan 25 · B / H
