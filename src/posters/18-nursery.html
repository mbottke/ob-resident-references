Birth medications

AAP vitamin K 2022 · CDC STI Guidelines 2021 · USPSTF 2019
Vitamin K (phytonadione)> 1,500 g: 1 mg IM within 6 h (0.5 mL of 1 mg/0.5 mL)
≤ 1,500 g: 0.3–0.5 mg/kg IM once. Not IV for prophylaxis.
Read the label: methylergonovine look-alike.
Eye prophylaxisErythromycin 0.5% ointment, both eyes, once, as soon as possible after birth (vaginal or cesarean); ≤ 24 h. Does not prevent chlamydial conjunctivitis.
RSV antibodyDose and maternal-vaccine rule 5 · E
Vitamin K refused: document; oral regimens protect less against late vitamin K deficiency bleeding (VKDB). Any bleeding < 6 mo → think VKDB.

06

Circumcision

AAP technical report 2012 (expired 2017, not replaced) · AAP 2015
Defer
Unstable or ill · significantly preterm · hypospadias, chordee, buried penis, penoscrotal webbing · blood dyscrasia or family history of bleeding disorder. Confirm vitamin K given.
DPNB
Dorsal penile nerve block: 1% (10 mg/mL) lidocaine, no epinephrine, 0.4 mL each side at the base (10 and 2 o’clock) = 8 mg total (2.7 mg/kg at 3 kg; 3.2 at 2.5 kg) → check the unit max
Ring block
1% lidocaine, no epinephrine, 0.8 mL at the base or midshaft; may be most effective (AAP). Instead of DPNB, not added
Adjuncts
Sucrose pacifier, swaddle, positioning — never the only analgesia. Topical 4% lidocaine is an option, not in low-birth-weight infants (skin irritation).
After
No excessive bleeding for ≥ 2 h before discharge.

Newborn discharge checklist

AAP COFN 2015 · term 37w0d–41w6d, uncomplicated

Clinical readiness complete dyad discharge 25 · A

  • Exam and course need no further stay
  • Vitals normal and stable for 12 h → 03 Vitals
  • Voiding regularly · ≥ 1 spontaneous stool
  • ≥ 2 successful feeds; a breastfeed observed and documented
  • Circumcision: no excessive bleeding ≥ 2 h → 06

Review and screens hearing + pending CMV owner 22 · H

  • Jaundice assessed with a plan 5 · C
  • EOS risk assessed per guideline 4 · EOS
  • Maternal syphilis, HBsAg, HIV reviewed → 05
  • Hepatitis B vaccine per risk → 02 Hepatitis B
  • Metabolic, hearing, pulse-ox screens done → 05 Screens

Home plan appointments / pending results 25 · H

  • Car seat; caregiver education: feeding, jaundice, safe sleep
  • Medical home; social risks assessed
  • Substance exposure: observe, family plan 24 · F 24 · J
  • Discharged at < 48 h of age → seen ≤ 48 h after discharge
