Delivery-note essentials

  • Date/time of birth · GA · G/P
  • Analgesia (epidural / none)
  • Presentation, position (OA/OP)
  • Delivery type; vacuum/forceps: indication, station, pulls, pop‑offs, duration
  • Nuchal cord (× __, reduced?)
  • Shoulder dystocia: head-to-body time, anterior shoulder, maneuvers in order
  • Weight, sex · supervisor present, role
  • Placenta: method · intact? · time
  • Cord: 3-vessel · delayed clamping · gases if sent 3 · D
  • QBL · uterotonics given
  • Laceration: degree (3a/b/c), suture, rectal exam before + after repair, counts
  • Apgars 1 + 5 min (q5 min to 20 if < 7) · if resuscitated: interventions + times
  • For EOS: Tmax · ROM h · GBS · antibiotic + hours before birth EOS

Calling the FM attending

admit to OB · not admitting 21

Open Name, role, unit · callback # · patient, room · how soon.

S

Situation G_P_ at __w_d (dating), chief complaint, why you’re calling.

B

Background GBS (+ antibiotic start time) · Rh · prior CS · oxytocin · neuraxial · pregnancy problems · meds/allergies.

A

Assessment Vitals · cervical exam (SVE: dilation / effacement / station) · ROM · FHR: B-V-A-D-T → Category 3 · A · trend · labs · actions + response. “My assessment / concern is ___.”

R

Recommendation “I recommend ___. I need you to ___ (now / by ___).” Then your questions. Confirm the plan, who owns care and when to reassess. Offer to write the H&P.

Inputs
