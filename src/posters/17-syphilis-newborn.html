01

Start hereSend at birth

CDC STI Guidelines 2021 · Congenital syphilis · collect these before choosing a scenario in 02

Mother

Quantitative RPR or VDRL at delivery — same test as the infant’s, ideally same lab.

No mother or baby leaves without maternal serology documented in pregnancy.

Infant

Quantitative nontreponemal test on infant serum. Not cord blood: maternal blood can give a false positive; Wharton’s jelly, a false negative.

No treponemal test on the infant (maternal IgG persists > 15 mo) · no IgM tests. HIV test for every at-risk neonate.

Exam · pathology

Exam: hydrops · direct hyperbilirubinemia (direct > 2 mg/dL or > 20% of total) or cholestasis · hepatosplenomegaly · rhinitis · rash · pseudoparalysis of a limb.

Placenta / cord to pathology: silver stain or T. pallidum PCR (CLIA-validated). Lesion or nasal discharge: darkfield or PCR. Stillbirth: skeletal survey.
