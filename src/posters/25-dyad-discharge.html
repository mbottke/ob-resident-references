  • Clinically ready: examination, course and required observation support home care; unresolved findings addressed.

MotherPrevention + emotional health

ACOG PB 181 (2017) · CDC (2024) · ACOG schedule (2026) · CPG 4 (2023) · AIM (2021) · details on 6
  • Rh: maternal antibody / RhD and newborn RhD results reconciled. Unsensitized RhD− mother + RhD+ newborn → RhIG within 72 h of birth; dose / fetomaternal hemorrhage testing 6 · F
  • Rubella / varicella: indicated postpartum vaccination completed; remaining doses arranged. RhIG does not delay indicated MMR / varicella vaccination. MMR given with RhIG / blood products → rubella immunity test ≥ 3 mo later when possible.
  • Tdap: pregnancy dose missed and never previously received Tdap → postpartum dose; otherwise review routine catch-up indications. Other indicated vaccines 6 · F
  • Contraception: preference, chosen method / start date and access addressed 6 · C
  • Validated depression / anxiety screen reviewed. Positive → assessment, with treatment / referral and follow-up arranged. Self-harm response → immediate risk assessment; psychosis → emergency assessment 6 · D

BothHome plan works for both

AAP safe sleep (2022) · car seats (2018 / 2009, reaff. 2025) · AHRQ RED Tool 3 (2013)
  • Feeding supplies, medicines, equipment, phone and transport available; barriers have a named solution.
  • Capable support identified; language / accessibility needs addressed.
  • Safe sleep: supine for every sleep; separate firm, flat sleep surface; fitted sheet only. No loose bedding / soft objects or bed sharing. Room-share at least 6 mo.

  • Born < 37 wk: car-seat observation 90–120 min or expected travel duration, whichever is longer.

J

Teach-back + document departure

AHRQ RED Toolkit Tool 3 (2013) · prompts and sign-off fields are proposed implementation
Ask patient / caregiver to explain or demonstrate. Gaps → explain again → repeat teach-back → resolve before departure.
  • “Show how medicines, BP checks and feeding will work at home.”
  • “Which changes need 911? Which need immediate assessment?”
  • “When and where is each patient’s next visit or test?”
  • “Who will contact you about results that are still pending?”
  • “What would you do if something felt seriously wrong, even if it is not on this list?”
  • Both charts document readiness, outstanding issues, discharge plans and receiving clinicians. Written instructions supplied in the preferred language / accessible format.
